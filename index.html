Meet The Attorney

Deborah J. Smiley

JD · Accredited Estate Planner® · Certified Financial Planner™

For 30 years, Deborah has handled estate and business planning, and estate and trust administration — trusts, business and corporate planning, real estate transactions, probate, and tax planning, all under one roof.

Throughout her law career, she spent eleven years in wealth management at trust companies, handling clients' financial planning directly. That's why the plan gets checked against your actual numbers, not a template.

She holds the Accredited Estate Planner® designation, awarded by the National Association of Estate Planners & Councils to professionals who meet its standards for experience, knowledge, and character.
