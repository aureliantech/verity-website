Background

Her Story

Deborah started practicing law in Missouri 30 years ago, focused on estate planning and probate. A few years in, she kept noticing the same gap: clients would leave her office with a legally sound will or trust, then go make financial decisions — retirement withdrawals, life insurance, business valuations — that quietly worked against the plan she'd just built.

So she became a Certified Financial Planner. For the past 11 years, she's practiced both — law and wealth management — side by side, specifically so that doesn't happen to her clients. She later added the AEP® (Accredited Estate Planner) designation, held by a small group of attorneys, financial planners, and CPAs who specialize in estate planning specifically.

Today, Deborah works with individuals and families on estate planning and trust administration, and with business owners on formation, contracts, and succession — always checking the legal side against the financial side, because in her experience, that's where most plans quietly break.
