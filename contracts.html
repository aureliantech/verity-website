What it doesn't do

What Contract Work Doesn't Do

Said plainly, so you know what else to plan for.

A contract makes a breach easier to prove and enforce. It doesn't prevent someone from breaching in the first place.

A well-drafted contract protects you within the terms you're agreeing to. It can't turn a deal that doesn't make sense into a good one.

If you have business partners, a buy-sell agreement has its own specific terms for what happens if one leaves.

Common Mistakes We Watch For

Most contract problems trace back to what happened before signing. Here's what we check for, every time.
